Passage

Hebrews 11

Verses 17–22 · WEB

Abraham, when tested, offered up Isaac, the son through whom the promise was to come, reasoning that God could raise him from the dead. Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph then acted by faith regarding things to come, blessing their sons and looking ahead to Israel’s exodus. Both traditions see faith that trusts God’s promise beyond death. They draw different lessons about testing.

Hebrews 11:17

17By faith, Abraham, being tested, offered up Isaac. Yes, he who had gladly received the promises was offering up his only born son,

Arminian

By faith Abraham, when tested, offered up Isaac. Arminian readers see a real test of Abraham’s faith, whose outcome mattered.

Reformed

Reformed readers see God testing Abraham to reveal and strengthen the faith he had given him.

Key contrast Both read the testing as real. Arminian readers see a genuine test of free response. Reformed readers see God displaying and refining faith that he himself had produced.

Hebrews 11:18

18to whom it was said, “Your offspring will be accounted as from Isaac,”

Arminian

He was told, “Through Isaac your offspring will be named.” Arminian readers see the tension between promise and command that faith held together.

Reformed

Reformed readers see God’s promise as unbreakable, even when his command seemed to contradict it.

Hebrews 11:19

19concluding that God is able to raise up even from the dead. Figuratively speaking, he also did receive him back from the dead.

Arminian

He considered that God was able to raise him from the dead; figuratively, he did receive him back. Arminian readers see faith in resurrection power.

Reformed

Reformed readers see Isaac’s return as a type of Christ’s resurrection.

Hebrews 11:20

20By faith Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau, even concerning things to come.

Arminian

By faith Isaac invoked future blessings on Jacob and Esau. Arminian readers see blessing tied to God’s promise.

Reformed

Reformed readers see God’s sovereign choice of Jacob over Esau carried out, as in Romans 9:10–13.

Hebrews 11:21

21By faith Jacob, when he was dying, blessed each of the sons of Joseph, and worshiped, leaning on the top of his staff.

Arminian

By faith Jacob, dying, blessed each of Joseph’s sons and worshiped. Arminian readers see faith that looks forward even at death.

Reformed

Reformed readers see God’s choice of the younger Ephraim reflecting his sovereign freedom.

Hebrews 11:22

22By faith Joseph, when his end was near, made mention of the departure of the children of Israel, and gave instructions concerning his bones.

Arminian

By faith Joseph, at the end of his life, spoke of the exodus and gave directions about his bones. Arminian readers see Joseph trusting God’s promise about the land.

Reformed

Reformed readers see confidence in God’s covenant faithfulness to bring his people home.
