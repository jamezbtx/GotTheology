Psalms 99

Verses 1–9 · ESV · Voices

The Lord reigns—let peoples tremble; he sits enthroned upon the cherubim—let the earth quake. The Lord is great in Zion, exalted over all peoples; let them praise his great and awesome name—he is holy. The King in his might loves justice; he has established equity and executed justice and righteousness in Jacob. Exalt the Lord our God; worship at his footstool—he is holy. Moses and Aaron were among his priests, Samuel among those who called on his name; they called, and he answered. He spoke in the pillar of cloud; they kept his testimonies and the statute he gave. O Lord our God, you answered them; you were a forgiving God to them, yet an avenger of their wrongdoings. Exalt the Lord our God; worship at his holy mountain—for the Lord our God is holy.

Psalms 99:1

1The Lord reigns; let the peoples tremble! He sits enthroned upon the cherubim; let the earth quake!

Arminian

The Lord reigns; peoples tremble; enthroned upon the cherubim; earth quake. Wesley: People—enemies; Sitteth—on the ark, present with his people; Earth—peoples; Moved—with fear. Shared: reign; cherubim throne; tremble/quake.

Arminian pastoral: presence at the ark is real. Trembling is fitting before enthroned holiness.

Reformed

Calvin: Jehovah reigns; peoples tremble; he dwells between the cherubim; earth moved—reign produces holy agitation, not casual familiarity.

Reformed pastoral: shared—reigns; cherubim; tremble/quake. Majesty shakes nations and ground alike.

Psalms 99:2

2The Lord is great in Zion; he is exalted over all the peoples.

Arminian

The Lord is great in Zion; exalted over all the peoples. Shared: Zion greatness; exaltation over peoples.

Arminian pastoral: local Zion, global height. Greatness in the church does not shrink worldwide supremacy.

Reformed

Calvin: Jehovah great in Zion; high above all peoples—Zion's God is not a tribal mascot.

Reformed pastoral: shared—great in Zion; exalted over peoples. Particular dwelling, universal highness.

Psalms 99:3

3Let them praise your great and awesome name! Holy is he!

Arminian

Let them praise your great and awesome name! Holy is he! Wesley: Them—all people. Shared: great/terrible name; holy is he.

Arminian pastoral: first holiness refrain. Awesome does not cancel praise—it deepens it.

Reformed

Calvin: they shall praise thy great and terrible name; it is holy—name and holiness bound.

Reformed pastoral: shared—great and awesome name; holy is he. Doxology starts with who he is.

Psalms 99:4

4The King in his might loves justice. You have established equity; you have executed justice and righteousness in Jacob.

Arminian

The King in his might loves justice; established equity; executed justice and righteousness in Jacob. Wesley: Judgment—absolute dominion managed in righteousness; King's strength—the strong king; Equity—in all proceedings. Shared: might loves justice; equity; Jacob's just rule.

Arminian pastoral: power without equity is not this King. Jacob experienced righteous administration—imitate it.

Reformed

Calvin: the king's strength loves judgment; thou established equity; executed judgment and righteousness in Jacob—irresistible might married to right.

Reformed pastoral: shared—might loves justice; equity; righteousness in Jacob. Throne ethics for the covenant people.

Psalms 99:5

5Exalt the Lord our God; worship at his footstool! Holy is he!

Arminian

Exalt the Lord our God; worship at his footstool! Holy is he! Wesley: Footstool—before the ark; Holy—pledge of presence. Shared: exalt; footstool worship; second holy refrain.

Arminian pastoral: footstool is low place for high God. Holiness again—do not rush past the refrain.

Reformed

Calvin: exalt Jehovah our God; worship at his footstool; he is holy—bowed approach at the ark's pledge.

Reformed pastoral: shared—exalt; footstool; holy is he. Low worship matches holy height.

Psalms 99:6

6Moses and Aaron were among his priests, Samuel also was among those who called upon his name. They called to the Lord, and he answered them.

Arminian

Moses and Aaron among his priests; Samuel among those who called on his name; they called, and he answered. Wesley: Moses—priestly work before formal priesthood; That call—solemn intercessors. Shared: priestly callers; answered prayer.

Arminian pastoral: history of answered intercession. Call as they called—expect the God who answers.

Reformed

Calvin: Moses and Aaron among his priests, Samuel among callers on his name; they called, and he answered—precedents of prevailing prayer.

Reformed pastoral: shared—priests; callers; he answered. Memory of answer fuels present asking.

Psalms 99:7

7In the pillar of the cloud he spoke to them; they kept his testimonies and the statute that he gave them.

Arminian

In the pillar of the cloud he spoke; they kept his testimonies and the statute he gave. Wesley: Spake—to Moses, Aaron, Samuel. Shared: cloudy speech; kept testimonies and statute.

Arminian pastoral: revelation and obedience pair. Keeping statute is how answered people live.

Reformed

Calvin: he spoke in the cloudy pillar; they kept testimonies and the statute he gave—word received and guarded.

Reformed pastoral: shared—spoke in cloud; kept testimonies and statute. Hearing without keeping is not the pattern.

Psalms 99:8

8O Lord our God, you answered them; you were a forgiving God to them, but an avenger of their wrongdoings.

Arminian

You answered them; a forgiving God to them, yet an avenger of their wrongdoings. Wesley: Them—intercessors; Forgavest—the people, sparing total destruction they deserved. Shared: answer; forgive; still avenge inventions/wrongdoings.

Arminian pastoral: pardon is real; discipline is real. Do not flatten holiness into cheap leniency or into refusal to forgive.

Reformed

Calvin: thou answeredst them; wast a God who forgavest them, though taking vengeance on their inventions—mercy to the people through intercession, yet not without chastening their corrupt ways.

Reformed pastoral: shared—answered; forgiving; avenger of wrongdoings. Forgiveness and holy vengeance are not enemies in God.

Psalms 99:9

9Exalt the Lord our God, and worship at his holy mountain; for the Lord our God is holy!

Arminian

Exalt the Lord our God; worship at his holy mountain; for the Lord our God is holy! Shared close: exalt; holy mountain; third holiness refrain.

Arminian pastoral: mountain worship ends where footstool began—exaltation. Holy closes the circle thrice.

Reformed

Calvin: exalt Jehovah our God; worship at his holy hill—for Jehovah our God is holy. Final holiness seals the reigning name.

Reformed pastoral: shared—exalt; holy mountain; our God is holy. Thrice-holy psalm ends in worship, not speculation.

Historic quotes

Voices from both camps

Historic words on the reigning Lord enthroned on the cherubim, a holy and awesome name, a King who loves justice in Jacob, worship at his footstool, Moses, Aaron, and Samuel answered, forgiveness joined to vengeance on inventions, and a thrice-holy close—so each camp speaks in its own voice beside Psalm 99:1–9.

Arminian Notable voices

“People—enemies of God and his people. Sitteth—upon the ark; present with his people. Earth—peoples of the earth; Moved—with fear. Them—all people. Judgment—though dominion absolute and power irresistible, he manages it with righteousness. King's strength—by Hebraism the strong king. Equity—in all proceedings. Footstool—before the ark (1 Chr 28:2). Holy—consecrated pledge of God's presence. Moses—executed priestly work before the formal priesthood (Exod 24:6). That call—who solemnly interceded for the people. Spake—to Moses and Aaron (Exod 19:24; 33:9–11) and to Samuel. Them—the intercessors. Forgavest—the people for whom they prayed, so far as not to destroy them utterly as they deserved.”
John Wesley · Explanatory Notes upon the Old Testament, Psalm 99

Reformed Notable voices

“Jehovah reigns; let the peoples tremble; he sits between the cherubim; let the earth be moved. Jehovah is great in Zion, high above all peoples. They shall praise thy great and terrible name—it is holy. The king's strength loves judgment; thou hast established equity; thou hast done judgment and righteousness in Jacob. Exalt Jehovah our God; worship at his footstool—he is holy. Moses and Aaron among his priests, Samuel among those who call on his name; they called, and he answered. He spoke to them in the cloudy pillar; they kept his testimonies and the statute he gave. Jehovah our God, thou answeredst them; thou wast a God who forgavest them, though taking vengeance on their inventions. Exalt Jehovah our God; worship at his holy hill—for Jehovah our God is holy. Holiness thrice marked; absolute power yoked to equity; answered intercession with pardon that still disciplines.”
John Calvin · Commentary on the Psalms, Psalm 99
