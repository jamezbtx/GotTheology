Passage

Acts 19

Verses 21–41 · WEB

Paul resolves in the Spirit to go through Macedonia and Achaia to Jerusalem and then to Rome. In Ephesus a silversmith named Demetrius, who makes shrines of Artemis, rouses the craftsmen by warning that Paul’s teaching threatens their trade and the goddess’s honor. The city fills with confusion, and the crowd rushes into the theater, shouting, “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!” for two hours. The town clerk finally quiets them, points out that the Christians have not robbed temples or blasphemed the goddess, directs any complaint to the courts, and dismisses the assembly.

Acts 19:21

21Now after these things had ended, Paul determined in the Spirit, when he had passed through Macedonia and Achaia, to go to Jerusalem, saying, “After I have been there, I must also see Rome.”

Arminian

Paul resolves in the Spirit to go to Jerusalem and then Rome. Arminian readers see Paul planning under the Spirit’s guidance.

Reformed

Reformed readers see the “must” of God’s plan taking Paul toward Rome.

Reformed

Reformed commentators see coordinated mission.

Acts 19:24
