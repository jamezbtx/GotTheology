Passage

Matthew 17

Verses 22–23 · ESV · Voices

Gathering in Galilee, Jesus again announces that the Son of Man will be delivered, killed, and raised on the third day. The disciples are greatly distressed—hearing the suffering more clearly than the promise of rising.

Matthew 17:22

22As they were gathering in Galilee, Jesus said to them, “The Son of Man is about to be delivered into the hands of men,

Arminian

In familiar Galilee Jesus restates the coming handover. “Delivered” signals betrayal and judicial violence ahead.

Arminian gospel weight: this delivery is for sinners. The Son freely goes toward the cross that grace proclaims as sufficient and sincerely offered to all who believe.

Reformed

The gathering becomes a classroom of the cross. “About to be delivered” is certainty, not rumor.

Reformed confession: the Son of Man’s path is appointed. Human hands will act; God’s purpose will stand.

Matthew 17:23

23and they will kill him, and he will be raised on the third day.” And they were greatly distressed.

Arminian

Death and third-day rising stand together, yet distress dominates the disciples’ hearing. Grief outruns hope for now.

Classical Arminians pastoralize both: the cross truly saves, and disciples must keep listening until resurrection joy catches up. Grace sustains those who stumble in understanding.

Reformed

Kill and raise—both are sure. Their great distress shows faith still partial; the promise is not yet embraced.

Reformed comfort: Christ’s word includes the third day. The Father’s plan holds death and victory together for the elect’s salvation.

Key contrast Both insist the prediction includes real death and real rising. Arminians typically stress the sincere gospel call flowing from this cross; Reformed readers typically stress the certainty of the decreed passion and resurrection for Christ’s people.

Historic quotes

Voices from both camps

Historic words on gathering in Galilee, Jesus again announces that the Son of Man will be delivered, killed, and raised on the third day—so each camp speaks in its own voice beside Matthew 17:22–23.

Arminian Notable voices

“They abode in Galilee - Lower Galilee, where the city of Capernaum was. The Son of man shall be betrayed into the hands of men - Μελλει - παραδιδοσθαι εις χειρας - The Son of man is about to be delivered into the hands, etc. I am fully of the mind of two eminent critics, Grotius and Wakefield, that παραδιδοσθαι should be here translated delivered, or delivered up, not betrayed; and that the agency, in this case, should be referred to God, not to Judas.”
Adam Clarke · Commentary on the New Testament (on Matt 17:22–23)

Reformed Notable voices

“And while they remained in Galilee, Jesus said to them, The Son of man will be delivered into the hands of men: 23. And they will kill him, and on the third day he will rise again. At that time the disciples came to Jesus, saying, Who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?”
John Calvin · Commentary on a Harmony of the Evangelists (on Matt 17:22–23)
