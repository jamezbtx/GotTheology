Isaiah 45

Verses 14–25 · ESV · Voices

After 45:1–13—Thus says the LORD to his anointed, to Cyrus, whose right hand I have grasped; potter and clay, woe to him who strives with his Maker; him stirred in righteousness to build my city and set my exiles free, not for price or reward—the word turns: the wealth of Egypt and the merchandise of Cush, and the Sabeans, men of stature, shall come over; Truly, you are a God who hides himself, O God of Israel, the Savior; Turn to me and be saved, all the ends of the earth.

Isaiah 45:14

14Thus says the LORD: “The wealth of Egypt and the merchandise of Cush, and the Sabeans, men of stature, shall come over to you and be yours; they shall follow you; they shall come over in chains and bow down to you. They will plead with you, saying: ‘Surely God is in you, and there is no other, no god besides him.’”

Arminian

Thus says the LORD: The wealth of Egypt and the merchandise of Cush, and the Sabeans, men of stature, shall come over to you and be yours. Wesley: The labour—The wealth gotten by their labour. Thee—Jerusalem shall not only be rebuilt, but the wealth and glory of other countries shall be brought to it again. This was in part verified in Jerusalem; but it was much more fully accomplished in the church of the gospel, in the accession of the Gentiles to that church which began in Jerusalem, and from thence spread itself into all the parts of the world. Come over—They shall be taken captive by thee, and willingly submit themselves to thee.

Arminian hearing: labour-wealth of Egypt, Cush, and Sabeans comes over to Jerusalem in part, and more fully to the gospel church as Gentiles accede from Jerusalem outward; they come over in chains and willingly submit. Shared loudly with Calvin: historic Egypt, Cush, and the Sabeans first, then the church; Surely God is in you, and there is no other. Historic nations, not a newspaper, not a living politician, not a millennial chart. Quote-honesty: Wesley’s “The labour—wealth gotten by their labour” is his lemma for ESV “The wealth of Egypt”; “Come over—taken captive, and willingly submit” is his lemma for “they shall come over in chains.” Prefer no keyContrast on 14: both camps take historic nations then the church; skip rather than a millennial fork. Do not confuse this with the Christ-Servant of 42:1–9; Wesley’s Messiah-as-Jehovah-and-man waits on v. 24’s “in the LORD,” not a rewrite of Cyrus. Match 45:13: city built and exiles freed, not for price. Match v. 20–22: survivors of the nations; Turn to me and be saved.

Reformed

Thus says the LORD: The wealth of Egypt and the merchandise of Cush, and the Sabeans, men of stature, shall come over. Calvin: still the restoration under Cyrus, but promises extend to the coming of Christ. Labour of Egypt / merchandise of Ethiopia: alludes to expenses Cyrus contributed for the temple (Ezra 6:8); Egypt and Ethiopia tributary to Persia. Beginning of homage nations later rendered to the Church after Christ. Historic Egypt, Cush, Sabeans first. Chains: government ascribed to the Church only as Christ’s spiritual scepter (gospel), Head and members; none bow to Christ without also obeying the Church so far as the obedience of faith. Surely God is in thee.

Reformed hearing: historic nations and temple-tribute first; gospel homage after Christ; chains are the spiritual scepter, not a millennial chart. Shared loudly with Wesley: historic Egypt, Cush, and the Sabeans, then the church; God is in you, no other. Historic nations, not a newspaper, not a dispensational timetable. Quote-honesty: Calvin’s “labour of Egypt / merchandise of Ethiopia” names ESV “wealth of Egypt and the merchandise of Cush.” Prefer no keyContrast on 14: both historic then church; skip rather than a millennial fork. Match 45:13: Cyrus, city, exiles not for price. Match v. 22: ends of the earth invited.

Isaiah 45:15

15Truly, you are a God who hides himself, O God of Israel, the Savior.

Arminian

Truly, you are a God who hides himself, O God of Israel, the Savior. Wesley: Verily—These are the words of the prophet, on contemplation of the various dispensations of God towards his church, and in the world. Hidest—Thy counsels are deep and incomprehensible, thy ways are past finding out.

Arminian hearing: the prophet speaks, contemplating various dispensations; hidden means counsels deep and incomprehensible, ways past finding out; still the Savior. Shared: Truly, you are a God who hides himself, O God of Israel, the Savior. Historic Savior of Israel, not a newspaper. Quote-honesty: Wesley’s “Verily” and “Hidest—counsels deep and incomprehensible” are his lemmas for ESV “Truly” and “hides himself.” Prefer no keyContrast on 15: Wesley’s incomprehensible counsels and Calvin’s withdraw-in-affliction overlap as hidden-yet-Savior; skip rather than invent a Christ-in-flesh fight Wesley never started. Match v. 14: God is in you, no other. Match v. 19: he did not speak in secret.

Reformed

Truly, you are a God who hides himself. Calvin: not Christ-hidden-in-flesh (too far-fetched for this verse); God appears to withdraw when he permits affliction; hope is not directed to things seen (Rom 8:24); Savior of Israel. Hidden assistance, need of patience.

Reformed hearing: hidden is withdraw-in-affliction and unseen hope, not an incarnation lemma here; assistance is hidden, patience needed; he remains Israel’s Savior. Shared with Wesley: a God who hides himself; O God of Israel, the Savior. Historic Savior, not a newspaper. Quote-honesty: Calvin’s “a God that hidest thyself” is his lemma for ESV “a God who hides himself.” Prefer no keyContrast: skip Calvin’s not-Christ-in-flesh rather than invent a Wesley fight. Match v. 14: Surely God is in you. Match v. 17: everlasting salvation still holds while he hides.

Isaiah 45:16

16All of them are put to shame and confounded; the makers of idols go in confusion together.

Arminian

All of them are put to shame and confounded; the makers of idols go in confusion together. Wesley has no extra lemma. Paraphrase from v. 15 honestly: these are the words of the prophet on contemplation of the various dispensations of God towards his church, and in the world; Hidest—counsels deep, ways past finding out. So here: All of them are put to shame and confounded; the makers of idols go in confusion together—one of those dispensations toward the world. Do not invent unique Wesley sentences.

Arminian hearing: idol-makers’ shame is a dispensation the prophet contemplates while God hides himself. Shared: makers of idols put to shame and confounded. Historic idol-makers, not a newspaper. Prefer no keyContrast: Wesley has no extra lemma; skip rather than invent. Match v. 15: various dispensations. Match v. 17: Israel is not put to shame. Match v. 20: wooden idols that cannot save.

Reformed

All of them are put to shame and confounded; the makers of idols go in confusion together. Calvin: idol-makers ashamed; do not judge God’s power from present prosperity of the wicked.

Reformed hearing: idol-makers go in confusion; present prosperity of the wicked is no measure of his power. Shared with Wesley: makers of idols put to shame. Historic idol-makers, not a newspaper. Quote-honesty: Calvin’s “idol-makers ashamed” is his lemma for “the makers of idols go in confusion together.” Prefer no keyContrast: Wesley has no extra lemma. Match v. 17: Israel saved while they are confounded. Match v. 20: a god that cannot save.

Isaiah 45:17

17But Israel is saved by the LORD with an everlasting salvation; you shall not be put to shame or confounded to all eternity.

Arminian

But Israel is saved by the LORD with an everlasting salvation; you shall not be put to shame or confounded to all eternity. Wesley has no extra lemma. Paraphrase from v. 14 honestly: Jerusalem rebuilt, and much more fully the church of the gospel, Gentiles acceding from Jerusalem outward. So here: Israel is saved by the LORD with an everlasting salvation; you shall not be put to shame or confounded to all eternity—that gospel church’s salvation. Do not invent unique Wesley sentences.

Arminian hearing: everlasting salvation is the LORD’s; Israel not confounded to all eternity; the gospel church of v. 14 is in view. Shared: Israel saved by the LORD with an everlasting salvation; not put to shame to all eternity. Historic Israel, not a newspaper, not a millennial chart. Prefer no keyContrast: Wesley has no extra lemma. No open theism: saved with an everlasting salvation already named. Match v. 14: church of the gospel. Match v. 16: idol-makers confounded; Israel is not. Match v. 25: offspring of Israel justified and glorying.

Reformed

But Israel is saved by the LORD with an everlasting salvation. Calvin: Israel saved with everlasting salvation. Do not judge God’s power from present prosperity of the wicked.

Reformed hearing: everlasting salvation is his; the wicked’s present prosperity does not measure it; Israel shall not be confounded to all eternity. Shared with Wesley: saved by the LORD with an everlasting salvation. Historic Israel, not a newspaper. Quote-honesty: Calvin’s “everlasting salvation” is the ESV lemma. Prefer no keyContrast: Wesley has no extra lemma. Match v. 16: idol-makers ashamed. Match v. 18: I am the LORD, and there is no other. Match v. 25: justified and glory in the LORD.

Isaiah 45:18

18For thus says the LORD, who created the heavens (he is God!), who formed the earth and made it (he established it; he did not create it empty, he formed it to be inhabited!): “I am the LORD, and there is no other.

Arminian

For thus says the LORD, who created the heavens (he is God!), who formed the earth and made it (he established it; he did not create it empty, he formed it to be inhabited!): I am the LORD, and there is no other. Wesley has no extra lemma. Paraphrase from v. 15 and v. 19 honestly: Hidest—counsels deep and incomprehensible; yet in v. 19 he has delivered his oracles to Israel publickly and plainly, not in vain. So here: I am the LORD, and there is no other—the same public God who formed the earth to be inhabited. Do not invent unique Wesley sentences.

Arminian hearing: maker of heavens and inhabited earth is the LORD, and there is no other; Wesley adds no creation-lemma of his own. Shared: I am the LORD, and there is no other; he formed the earth to be inhabited. Historic Maker, not a newspaper. Prefer no keyContrast: skip Calvin’s church-cannot-fail-while-earth-endures rather than invent Wesley prose. No open theism: he is God; he established it. Match 45:5–6: I am the LORD, and there is no other. Match v. 19: I the LORD speak the truth.

Reformed

For thus says the LORD, who created the heavens (he is God!), who formed the earth and made it. Calvin: Jehovah maker of the earth; argument from less to greater; earth formed to be inhabited; Church cannot fail while earth endures. I am Jehovah, none else.

Reformed hearing: from inhabited earth to the Church that cannot fail while earth endures; I am Jehovah, none else. Shared with Wesley: I am the LORD, and there is no other; formed to be inhabited. Historic Maker, not a newspaper, not a millennial chart. Quote-honesty: Calvin’s “formed it to be inhabited” is the ESV lemma. Prefer no keyContrast: Wesley has no extra lemma; skip rather than invent. Match 45:12: I made the earth. Match v. 17: everlasting salvation. Match v. 21: none besides me.

Isaiah 45:19

19I did not speak in secret, in a land of darkness; I did not say to the offspring of Jacob, ‘Seek me in vain.’ I the LORD speak the truth; I declare what is right.

Arminian

I did not speak in secret, in a land of darkness; I did not say to the offspring of Jacob, Seek me in vain. I the LORD speak the truth; I declare what is right. Wesley: In secret—The Heathen idols deliver oracles in obscure cells and caverns: but I have delivered my oracles to Israel publickly and plainly. In vain—Serve and worship me for nought. As I appointed them work, so from time to time I have given them abundant recompence. Right—I require nothing of my people which is not highly just and good.

Arminian hearing: heathen oracles in cells and caverns; his oracles public and plain; not in vain—work appointed, recompence given; he requires what is just and good. Shared: I did not speak in secret; I did not say, Seek me in vain; I the LORD speak the truth; I declare what is right. Historic oracles to Israel, not a newspaper. Quote-honesty: Wesley’s “In secret—obscure cells and caverns” is his lemma for “I did not speak in secret, in a land of darkness”; “In vain—Serve and worship me for nought” is his lemma for “Seek me in vain.” No open theism: he speaks the truth; he declares what is right. Prefer no keyContrast: both camps hear public word versus heathen hiding. Match v. 15: he hides himself in counsel, not in a dark oracle-cell. Match v. 18: I am the LORD, and there is no other. Match v. 21: who declared it of old.

Reformed

I did not speak in secret, in a land of darkness. Calvin: not in secret—unlike heathen groves and tripod oracles. Word is lamp (Ps 119; 2 Pet 1:19). Seek me not in vain. Speaks righteousness.

Reformed hearing: public word versus groves and tripod; the word is a lamp; he does not send Jacob to seek in vain; he speaks righteousness. Shared with Wesley: not in secret; not in vain; public versus heathen oracles. Historic word to Jacob, not a newspaper. Quote-honesty: Calvin’s “not in secret” and “Seek me not in vain” are his lemmas for the ESV. Prefer no keyContrast: Wesley’s cells-and-caverns and Calvin’s groves-and-tripod overlap. Match v. 18: I am the LORD. Match v. 21: who told this long ago.

Isaiah 45:20

20“Assemble yourselves and come; draw near together, you survivors of the nations! They have no knowledge who carry about their wooden idols, and keep on praying to a god that cannot save.

Arminian

Assemble yourselves and come; draw near together, you survivors of the nations! They have no knowledge who carry about their wooden idols, and keep on praying to a god that cannot save. Wesley: Draw near—To hear what I have said, and am now about to say. Of the nations—The remnant of the Gentiles, who survive the many destructions, which I am bringing upon the Heathen nations.

Arminian hearing: draw near to hear; survivors are the remnant of the Gentiles who survive destructions on the heathen nations; wooden idols cannot save. Shared: assemble, survivors of the nations; wooden idols; a god that cannot save. Historic nations, not a newspaper, not a millennial chart. Quote-honesty: Wesley’s “Draw near—to hear” and “Of the nations—remnant of the Gentiles” are his lemmas for “draw near together, you survivors of the nations.” Prefer no keyContrast: both hear historic Gentile survivors. Match v. 14: Egypt, Cush, Sabeans. Match v. 16: makers of idols confounded. Match v. 22: all the ends of the earth.

Reformed

Assemble yourselves and come; draw near together, you survivors of the nations. Calvin: assemble, escaped of the nations—survivors of the Gentiles / those of no value in God’s sight though high among men. Carry wood of graven image; pray to a god that cannot save.

Reformed hearing: escaped Gentiles assembled; high among men, no value in his sight if they carry wood and pray to a god that cannot save. Shared with Wesley: survivors of the nations; wooden idols that cannot save. Historic Gentiles, not a newspaper. Quote-honesty: Calvin’s “escaped of the nations” is his lemma for ESV “survivors of the nations.” Prefer no keyContrast. Match v. 14: historic nations come over. Match v. 21: present your case. Match v. 22: Turn to me and be saved.

Isaiah 45:21

21Declare and present your case; let them take counsel together! Who told this long ago? Who declared it of old? Was it not I, the LORD? And there is no other god besides me, a righteous God and a Savior; there is none besides me.

Arminian

Declare and present your case; let them take counsel together! Who told this long ago? Who declared it of old? Was it not I, the LORD? And there is no other god besides me, a righteous God and a Savior; there is none besides me. Wesley: Take counsel—To maintain the cause of their idols. This—This great work, Babylon’s destruction, and the redemption of God’s people.

Arminian hearing: counsel is to maintain the idols’ cause; “this” is Babylon’s destruction and the people’s redemption; it was the LORD who told it long ago; a righteous God and a Savior, none besides. Shared: who declared it of old was the LORD; no other god besides me; a righteous God and a Savior. Historic Babylon and redemption, not a newspaper. Quote-honesty: Wesley’s “Take counsel—maintain the cause of their idols” and “This—Babylon’s destruction, and the redemption of God’s people” are his lemmas for “let them take counsel together” and “Who told this long ago.” No open theism: who declared it of old was the LORD; he is not learning the case. Prefer no keyContrast: shared monotheism and declared-of-old. Match v. 19: I declare what is right. Match v. 22: Turn to me and be saved. Match v. 23: the word shall not return.

Reformed

Declare and present your case; let them take counsel together. Calvin: challenge idolaters. Who proclaimed from the beginning? Babylon’s fall and redemption of the people. A just God and a Savior.

Reformed hearing: idolaters challenged; the long-ago proclamation is Babylon’s fall and the people’s redemption; he is a just God and a Savior, none besides. Shared with Wesley: this is Babylon and redemption; no other god besides me; a righteous God and a Savior. Historic Babylon, not a newspaper. Quote-honesty: Calvin’s “Who proclaimed from the beginning” is his lemma for “Who told this long ago? Who declared it of old?” No open theism: it was I, the LORD, who declared it of old. Prefer no keyContrast: 45:21–22 shared monotheism. Match v. 18: I am the LORD, and there is no other. Match v. 23: I have sworn by myself.

Isaiah 45:22

Turn to me and be saved, all the ends of the earth! For I am God, and there is no other. Wesley has no extra lemma. Hear the verse; shared monotheism. Do not invent unique Wesley sentences. So here: Turn to me and be saved, all the ends of the earth! For I am God, and there is no other—the same public LORD of v. 19 and the same none-besides of v. 21.

Arminian hearing: hear the verse; ends of the earth turn and are saved because he is God and there is no other. Shared loudly with Calvin: Turn to me and be saved, all the ends of the earth; I am God, and there is no other. Historic nations and the ends of the earth, not a newspaper, not a millennial timetable. Prefer no keyContrast on 22: shared monotheism; do not fork this verse. Match v. 14: historic nations come over. Match v. 20: survivors of the nations. Match v. 21: a righteous God and a Savior. Match v. 23: every knee, all nations.

Reformed hearing: the whole world invited; Gentiles called; wall broken; look with the eye of faith; he is God and there is none else. Isaiah 45:23

23By myself I have sworn; from my mouth has gone out in righteousness a word that shall not return: ‘To me every knee shall bow, every tongue shall swear allegiance.’

Arminian

By myself I have sworn; from my mouth has gone out in righteousness a word that shall not return: To me every knee shall bow, every tongue shall swear allegiance. Wesley: In righteousness—It is what I will faithfully perform. Return—Without effect. It is a metaphor from ambassadors, who sometimes return to their princes without any success in their business. Every tongue—Not only the Jews, but all nations.

Arminian hearing: the sworn word is what he will faithfully perform; it does not return without effect, unlike failed ambassadors; every tongue is not only the Jews but all nations. Shared: By myself I have sworn; the word shall not return; every knee shall bow, every tongue shall swear. Historic nations, not a newspaper, not a millennial camp fight. Quote-honesty: Wesley’s “Return—Without effect” and “Every tongue—Not only the Jews, but all nations” are his lemmas for “a word that shall not return” and “every tongue shall swear allegiance.” No open theism: he swore; the word shall not return. Prefer no keyContrast on 23: Wesley’s all-nations and Calvin’s every-knee overlap; Calvin’s Rom 14 is the course of Christ’s kingdom till perfection, not a camp fight. Match v. 22: ends of the earth. Match v. 21: who declared it of old.

Reformed

By myself I have sworn; from my mouth has gone out in righteousness a word that shall not return. Calvin: I have sworn by myself. Gentiles admitted to equality with Jews; God common Father. Word shall not return. Every knee shall bow, every tongue swear. Paul applies to the last judgment (Rom 14:10–11), but statements relating to the kingdom of Christ extend to its whole course till full perfection—not a millennial camp fight.

Reformed hearing: sworn by himself; Gentiles equal with Jews; the word does not return; every knee and tongue; Rom 14 names the last judgment, yet the kingdom’s whole course till perfection, not a millennial fork. Shared with Wesley: every knee; all nations; the word shall not return. Historic nations, not a newspaper. Quote-honesty: Calvin’s “Every knee shall bow, every tongue swear” is his lemma for the ESV. No open theism: I have sworn; the word shall not return. Prefer no keyContrast on 23: overlap with Wesley’s “not only the Jews, but all nations”; skip rather than invent a camp fight. Match v. 22: Turn to me. Match v. 24: only in the LORD, righteousness and strength.

Isaiah 45:24

24“Only in the LORD, it shall be said of me, are righteousness and strength; to him shall come and be ashamed all who were incensed against him.

Arminian

Only in the LORD, it shall be said of me, are righteousness and strength; to him shall come and be ashamed all who were incensed against him. Wesley: In the Lord—By or from God alone, or the Messiah, who is the true Jehovah as well as man. Righteousness—To justify me from all things which I could not be justified by the law of Moses. Strength—Support and assistance to bear all my burdens, overcome all my enemies, and perform all my duties. Men—The Gentiles shall come to Christ. Ashamed—But all his implacable enemies shall be brought to shame.

Arminian hearing: “In the LORD” is by or from God alone, or the Messiah, true Jehovah as well as man; righteousness justifies from what Moses’ law could not; strength bears burdens and duties; Gentiles come to Christ; implacable enemies ashamed. Shared: only in the LORD are righteousness and strength; the incensed are ashamed. Historic confession, not a newspaper, not a millennial chart. Quote-honesty: Wesley’s “In the Lord—By or from God alone, or the Messiah, who is the true Jehovah as well as man” is his lemma for ESV “Only in the LORD”; this is not a rewrite of Cyrus or of the Christ-Servant of 42:1–9. Match v. 23: every knee. Match v. 25: justified and glory.

Reformed

Only in the LORD, it shall be said of me, are righteousness and strength. Calvin: righteousness and strength the two principal parts of salvation; both from God; in themselves they have neither. To him shall he come: access by faith. Rebels ashamed. Calvin does not here name “Messiah, Jehovah as well as man” the way Wesley does.

Reformed hearing: righteousness and strength both from Jehovah; they who confess their righteousness is placed in God approach him by faith; in themselves they have neither; rebels who defy him shall be ashamed. Shared with Wesley: only in the LORD are righteousness and strength; the incensed are ashamed. Historic confession, not a newspaper, not a millennial chart. Quote-honesty: Calvin’s “righteousness and strength” names the ESV lemma; he does not here make “in the LORD” a Messiah-as-Jehovah-and-man title. Match v. 23: sworn word. Match v. 25: justified and glory in the LORD, not works.

Key contrast Arminian readings, with Wesley, take “In the LORD” as by or from God alone, or the Messiah, who is the true Jehovah as well as man; righteousness to justify from all things the law of Moses could not; Gentiles shall come to Christ. Reformed readings, with Calvin, take righteousness and strength as both from Jehovah, the two principal parts of salvation; they who confess their righteousness is placed in God approach him by faith; in themselves they have neither; rebels who defy him shall be ashamed. Shared: only in the LORD are righteousness and strength; the incensed are ashamed; historic confession, not a millennial chart.

Reformed Notable voices

“Still the restoration under Cyrus, but promises extend to the coming of Christ. Labour of Egypt / merchandise of Ethiopia: alludes to expenses Cyrus contributed for the temple (Ezra 6:8); Egypt and Ethiopia tributary to Persia. Beginning of homage nations later rendered to the Church after Christ. Historic Egypt, Cush, and Sabeans first. Chains: government ascribed to the Church only as Christ’s spiritual scepter (gospel), Head and members; none bow to Christ without also obeying the Church so far as the obedience of faith. Surely God is in thee. Truly thou art a God that hidest thyself: not Christ-hidden-in-flesh (too far-fetched); God appears to withdraw when he permits affliction; hope is not directed to things seen (Rom 8:24); Savior of Israel; hidden assistance, need of patience. Idol-makers ashamed; Israel saved with everlasting salvation. Do not judge God’s power from present prosperity of the wicked. Jehovah maker of the earth: argument from less to greater; earth formed to be inhabited; Church cannot fail while earth endures. I am Jehovah, none else. Not in secret: unlike heathen groves and tripod oracles. Word is lamp (Ps 119; 2 Pet 1:19). Seek me not in vain. Speaks righteousness. Assemble, escaped of the nations: survivors of the Gentiles / those of no value in God’s sight though high among men. Carry wood of graven image; pray to a god that cannot save. Tell ye: challenge idolaters. Who proclaimed from the beginning? Babylon’s fall and redemption of the people. A just God and a Savior. Look unto me and be saved, all the ends of the earth. Invites the whole world; calling of the Gentiles; partition-wall broken (Eph 2:14). Look with the eye of faith; John 3:16. For I am God and there is none else. I have sworn by myself. Gentiles admitted to equality with Jews; God common Father. Word shall not return. Every knee shall bow, every tongue swear. Paul applies to the last judgment (Rom 14:10–11), but statements relating to the kingdom of Christ extend to its whole course till full perfection. Surely in Jehovah: righteousness and strength the two principal parts of salvation; both from God; in themselves they have neither. To him shall he come: access by faith. Rebels ashamed. In Jehovah shall all the seed of Israel be justified and glory. Seed extended: Gentiles ingrafted counted children of Abraham (Isa 44:5). Justified and glory in the Lord, not works (Rom 3:27; 4:2).”
John Calvin · Commentary on Isaiah (on Isa 45:14–25)
