Passage

Revelation 13

Verses 11–18 · WEB

A second beast rises from the earth, with horns like a lamb but speaking like a dragon. He makes the earth worship the first beast, performs signs, and sets up a speaking image. He forces all people to receive a mark on the right hand or forehead, without which no one can buy or sell. The number of the beast is 666.

Revelation 13:11

11I saw another beast coming up out of the earth. He had two horns like a lamb and it spoke like a dragon.

Arminian

The second beast looks like a lamb but speaks like a dragon. Later called the false prophet (16:13), he is often seen as religious or propaganda power serving the state.

Reformed

Reformed readers see false religion that imitates Christ while serving Satan.

Revelation 13:12

12He exercises all the authority of the first beast in his presence. He makes the earth and those who dwell in it to worship the first beast, whose fatal wound was healed.

Arminian

He makes the earth worship the first beast.

Reformed

Reformed readers see religion used to enforce idolatry.

Revelation 13:13

13He performs great signs, even making fire come down out of the sky to the earth in the sight of people.

Arminian

He performs great signs, even fire from heaven, imitating Elijah.

Reformed

Reformed readers see that miracles alone do not prove truth (Deuteronomy 13).

Revelation 13:14

14He deceives my own people who dwell on the earth because of the signs he was granted to do in front of the beast, saying to those who dwell on the earth that they should make an image to the beast who had the sword wound and lived.

Arminian

He deceives those who dwell on the earth.

Reformed

Reformed readers see deception as permitted by God and effective on those who do not love the truth (2 Thessalonians 2:10–11).

Revelation 13:15

15It was given to him to give breath to the image of the beast, that the image of the beast should both speak, and cause as many as wouldn’t worship the image of the beast to be killed.

Arminian

He gives breath to the beast’s image and has those who refuse to worship it killed.

Reformed

Reformed readers see the cost of faithfulness under idolatrous power.

Revelation 13:16

16He causes all, the small and the great, the rich and the poor, and the free and the slave, to be given marks on their right hands or on their foreheads;

Arminian

All are forced to receive a mark on the right hand or forehead. Interpreters debate whether the mark is literal or a symbol of loyalty, as God’s seal on the forehead is. Many note the parallel with the sealing of God’s servants in chapter 7.

Reformed

Reformed readers usually see the mark as a symbol of allegiance contrasted with God’s seal.

Revelation 13:17

17and that no one would be able to buy or to sell unless he has that mark, which is the name of the beast or the number of his name.

Arminian

No one can buy or sell without it, which pictures economic pressure to conform.

Reformed

Reformed readers see economic exclusion as a form of persecution.

Revelation 13:18

18Here is wisdom. He who has understanding, let him calculate the number of the beast, for it is the number of a man. His number is six hundred sixty-six.

Arminian

The number 666 has been calculated in many ways. A common view is that it represents Nero Caesar in Hebrew letters; others see a symbol of human failure falling short of seven. Arminian and Reformed interpreters across schools caution against speculative identification of contemporary figures.

Reformed

Reformed readers note both the Nero calculation and the symbolic reading. The call for wisdom is a call for discernment, not date-setting.

About these notes. Commentary here is pastoral and educational. It is not a substitute for reading primary sources, confessions, or trusted teachers in either tradition. Both Arminian and Reformed families contain internal diversity (Wesleyan, classical Remonstrant, confessional Calvinist, and more). GotTheology aims for fair representation—not a final verdict.
