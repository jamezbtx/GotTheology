Passage

Revelation 14

Verses 14–20 · WEB

One like a son of man sits on a white cloud with a sickle, and the earth is harvested. Another angel gathers the grapes of the earth into the great winepress of God’s wrath, and blood flows from the press for a great distance.

Revelation 14:14

14I looked, and saw a white cloud, and on the cloud one sitting like a son of man, having on his head a golden crown, and in his hand a sharp sickle.

Arminian

One like a son of man on a cloud is most likely Christ (Daniel 7:13).

Reformed

Reformed readers see Christ as the one who brings the harvest.

Revelation 14:15

15Another angel came out of the temple, crying with a loud voice to him who sat on the cloud, “Send your sickle and reap, for the hour to reap has come; for the harvest of the earth is ripe!”

Arminian

An angel calls for the harvest because it is ripe.

Reformed

Reformed readers see God’s appointed time.

Revelation 14:16

16He who sat on the cloud thrust his sickle on the earth, and the earth was reaped.

Arminian

The earth is harvested. Some see the gathering of the righteous, others judgment.

Reformed

Reformed readers note both readings, with many seeing the gathering of God’s people.

Revelation 14:17

17Another angel came out of the temple which is in heaven. He also had a sharp sickle.

Arminian

Another angel has a sharp sickle.

Reformed

Reformed readers see preparation for judgment.

Revelation 14:18

18Another angel came out from the altar, he who has power over fire, and he called with a great voice to him who had the sharp sickle, saying, “Send your sharp sickle and gather the clusters of the vine of the earth, for the earth’s grapes are fully ripe!”

Arminian

An angel with power over fire calls for the grape harvest.

Reformed

Reformed readers link the fire to the altar and the prayers of the saints.

Revelation 14:19

19The angel thrust his sickle into the earth, and gathered the vintage of the earth and threw it into the great wine press of the wrath of God.

Arminian

The grapes are thrown into the winepress of God’s wrath, recalling Isaiah 63 and Joel 3.

Reformed

Reformed readers see final judgment.

Revelation 14:20

20The wine press was trodden outside of the city, and blood came out of the wine press, up to the bridles of the horses, as far as one thousand six hundred stadia.

Arminian

Blood flows up to horses’ bridles for 1,600 stadia. Most read the number symbolically, as a judgment covering the whole land.

Reformed

Reformed readers see the completeness of judgment.

About these notes. Commentary here is pastoral and educational. It is not a substitute for reading primary sources, confessions, or trusted teachers in either tradition. Both Arminian and Reformed families contain internal diversity (Wesleyan, classical Remonstrant, confessional Calvinist, and more). GotTheology aims for fair representation—not a final verdict.
