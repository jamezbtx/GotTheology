# Proverbs verse ranges (WEB)

Proposed draft plan: 31 chapters, 79 ranges.

Paths: `content/books/proverbs/<ch>/<start>-<end>.json` (hyphens in filenames; en-dashes in display titles).

Draft only. No build, push, deploy, landing update, sitemap change, or indexed-set addition.
AdSense is not approved yet: these books stay noindex and out of the sitemap.
A `_draft` marker in this book folder keeps the book out of a default site build until a later local-build / publish step.

Chapter endings verified from the official eBible.org WEB numbered chapter HTML, not inferred from array length.
Official counts match `en_web.json` for this book (no numbering gaps).

Special rule for Proverbs: use the planned topical / section units. Do not write one file per verse or per individual proverb.
Keep collection boundaries: 1–9 discourses; 10:1–22:16 Solomonic sentences; 22:17–24:22 words of the wise; 24:23–34 further sayings; 25–29 Hezekiah collection; 30 Agur; 31 Lemuel and the 31:10–31 acrostic (keep the acrostic as one file).
